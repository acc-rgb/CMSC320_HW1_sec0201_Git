Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: As a Geospatial Data Science major, I'd say GIS is a pretty applicable field. Theres a lot of locational data that is used. For example, lets say we want to analyze population growth
in regions surrounding College Park. Taking temporal data from the past 50 years, we can track the average change in population for surrounding towns every 10 years, visualizing the data in 
graphs that highlight which towns increase in population, which decrease, and where a general trend in movement is.
